Our Team

AI Hydrological Forecasting Research Group

Focused on AI-based hydrological forecasting, with particular emphasis on leveraging hydrological big data and deeply coupling hydrological physical mechanisms with deep learning.

Part of the Water Resources and Flood Control Innovation Team, Dalian University of Technology.
